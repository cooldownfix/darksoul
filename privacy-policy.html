Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Darksoul Ascent. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website at darksoulascent.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only anonymised analytics data (page views, country of origin, device type) through Cloudflare's built-in analytics. We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party tracking scripts. We do not collect names, email addresses or personal identifiers unless you voluntarily submit a review on this page.

3. Cookies

We use a single first-party cookie to remember your cookie consent preference. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or cross-site identifiers.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. Cloudflare acts as our infrastructure and DNS provider; their privacy policy applies to network-level data.

5. Data Retention

Consent preferences stored in localStorage are retained on your device until you clear your browser data. No personal data is stored on our servers.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request deletion of any data associated with you. As we do not store personally identifiable information, there is typically nothing to delete. Contact us at privacy@darksoulascent.com with any requests.

7. Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

Questions? Email us at privacy@darksoulascent.com
