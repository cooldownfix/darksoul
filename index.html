About the Game

In the shattered realm of Vaelmoor, the dead do not rest. You are the Darksoul — an ancient wraith stripped of form, bound to wander the ruins of a world that has forgotten your name.

To reclaim your essence, you must ascend through the Forgotten Crypt, scale the Splintered Peak, and face the corrupted gods who sealed your fate. Every defeat carves a lesson into your spectral core.

Master Spectral Blast, Shadow Dash and Void Angess — abilities drawn from the souls of fallen enemies. Adapt. Evolve. Ascend.
